Home is a fast, minimalist Android launcher built with Jetpack Compose, designed for focus and privacy.

Features:
• Minimalist Compose UI: Edge-to-edge layout where pinned apps smoothly scroll under the status bar and behind the search bar docked directly above your keyboard.
• Fuzzy Search: Tolerates typos and partial queries; press Enter to instantly launch the top match.
• Pinned Apps & Groups: Long-press in search to pin/unpin. Drag-and-drop favorites to reorder, merge up to 4 apps into a grouped row, or extract an app from a group with smooth physics-based spring animations.
• Notification Shade Gesture: Swipe down anywhere over the pinned apps area to quickly pull down the system notification shade.
• Resilient Work Profile Support: Seamless multi-profile support with work badges and isolated launching. Pinned apps from locked or paused profiles are safely preserved rather than discarded.
• Instant Launch & Smart Caching: Asynchronous app discovery with multi-tier on-demand icon caching (memory LRU and disk cache) to eliminate startup stutter and prevent icon pop-in.
• Material You: Dynamic colors adapting to your wallpaper on Android 12+, with full light and dark theme support.
• Privacy-First: No internet access permission, no ads, no trackers, and no QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.
